Who we are

Tru Pharma is a newly established, partner-led company based in Wakefield, West Yorkshire. Our directors, including Director Saikiran Koneru, are building a wholesale pharmaceutical distribution business for the UK healthcare sector.

We are in our set-up phase, putting the people, premises, quality systems and supplier relationships in place before we begin to trade.

Our mission

To provide reliable, honest supply to pharmacies and healthcare providers.

The “Tru” in our name is our promise: truthful about what we do, consistent in how we do it, and worthy of the trust that patients and professionals place in the medicines supply chain.

What we do

Once authorised, we intend to source medicines and healthcare products from approved suppliers and distribute them to pharmacies, hospitals and other licensed partners in the UK.

Source

Work with authorised suppliers and manufacturers, verifying credentials before we buy.

Store and handle

Appropriate storage conditions, stock control and documentation at every stage.

Distribute

Dependable, traceable delivery to licensed customers who have been verified.

Our commitment to compliance

Tru Pharma is committed to full compliance with UK regulatory standards, including Good Distribution Practice (GDP). We are currently progressing our Wholesale Distribution Authorisation (WDA(H)) application with the MHRA.

We do not currently hold a WDA(H) and are not trading or supplying medicines. We will begin only once authorisation is granted.
